How it works · stylist poll key

Pick from "choose a stylist" on any card, or open the stylist list with the › tab on the left and drag a stylist onto an influencer. On a phone, swipe the stylist to the right, or press and hold, then drag. Tap a photo for her latest posts and reels. Hair notes come from her latest Instagram photos, so double-check before the day. Sidebar counts are per slot (6 · 7 · 8 PM); orange means 3 or more in one hour.

Before you start

Type your full name so we know who allocated each stylist. This device will remember your name, and every stylist you allocate is saved under it for the whole team to see.
